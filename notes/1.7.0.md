# Item mall supplies, battle scrolls, reverse scroll restock
- New, off by default, under Supplies: buy HP potion bags from the Item Mall when the total HP stock runs low, bounded by a per-bot-run silk budget. One order at a time lands straight in the bag while hunting; the merchant town trips stay the primary supply, a refusal backs that item off for a minute, and an unconfirmed order blocks all mall buying until you re-enter the game (the game's own resync rule — it is never retried).
- Battle scrolls: keep a 20% damage increase and/or a 20% resistance scroll active while hunting. Uses your bag stock and re-uses a scroll whenever the 30-minute buff runs out; both can also be topped up from the mall under the same budget.
- Reverse Return Scrolls can now be refilled at the recorded merchant with gold, like the potions; a merchant without them skips them with a notice and the trip still walks back.
- The read-only mall probe stays available as `SROLean.mall`; the buyer and the scrolls are exposed as `SROLean.mallBuyer` and `SROLean.scrolls` for live inspection.
> The catalogue read (mall-open, balances, 103 real offers) was verified live on the server; the buying itself (mall-buy) is verified in simulation only and has not yet spent real silk.
