# Party: accept list, party leader name and leave-if-wrong-leader
- New **Accept only these players** box on the Party page. Auto-accept then answers only applicants whose character name is on the list (comma separated, any letter case). Empty means everyone, as before. The list is saved per character.
- **Main account character name** is now **Party leader name**, and the option reads **Join the party leader's party**. Your saved name carries over.
- New option **Leave the party if its leader is someone else**: for your other accounts, the bot leaves a party whose leader is not the named character, then looks for the named leader's party as usual.
- Not included: inviting players from a list. Auto-recruit still works through the party-matching listing.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server.
