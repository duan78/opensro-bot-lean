# Lure mode for tanks, thanks to Rebraxin
- New **Lure** section (Combat → Hunt area, and the classic Training place page). A tank walks to saved pull points in turn, taunts the nearest monster, brings it back to the hunting spot, pauses on the way so it keeps up, then holds while the party takes over. It never attacks. Off by default. Setup is in the manual under "Luring monsters to the party".
- New setting **Stop pulling at this many monsters in the training area**: while that many monsters are already at the hunting spot, the tank waits instead of pulling more. 0 turns it off.
> Lure mode was run on the live server by a player with a Warrior tank in a four-character party, who reported it works. The stop-pulling setting is tested in simulation only.
