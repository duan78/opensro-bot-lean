# Auto Quest, second pass

- Repeatable quests are taken again: after completing one, its NPC's new offer marker is answered like any other. The server's marker decides what can be accepted now.
- The Quest page's journal rows gain an Abandon button (with a confirm click) that sends the game's own give-up command once and settles on the journal's answer. Skipping keeps a quest out without dropping its progress.
- Journal rows also say "targets not around" while hunting when a quest's learned monsters have not spawned near, so a quest that needs another hunting spot is visible at a glance.
- Quest mechanics that need their own in-world actions are not automated: gathering with a quest tool (stand in its area and use the knife), and capture traps (cast the quest's trap skill on its monster, e.g. via your rotation). Accepting and turning those quests in is still automatic.
- Still simulation-tested only; the quest opcodes and the dialogue flow have not been verified on the live server yet.
