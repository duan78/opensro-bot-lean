# Buff weapon swaps work with a full bag when no shield has to come off
- Swapping to a buff weapon exchanges places with the weapon in your bag, so it needs no free bag slot. The bot used to refuse to start with a completely full bag. It now starts whenever no shield (or arrows) has to be taken off, and only asks for one free slot when one does. The message says so ("free bag slot to take off your shield").
- Town trips and the rest of the nearly-full-bag handling are unchanged. If the character still gets stuck with one slot left, send the exact message the bot shows.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server.
