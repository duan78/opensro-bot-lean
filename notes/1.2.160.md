# F6 minimises and restores the panel
- F6 now does what the × does: it minimises the panel to the small icon, and a second F6 brings it back. The bot keeps running either way.
- F6 also brings back a panel hidden with Shift+F7.
- Ignored while typing in a field or when the key is held down, and the game never sees the key.
> Tested in simulation and on a test page, not in a live game.
