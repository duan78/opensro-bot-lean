# Buffs now wear the weapon they need
- With **Swap weapons for buffs** on, a buff your current weapon cannot cast is now cast with a matching weapon from your bag, picked from the skill's own weapon requirement. No saved buff set is needed. Your combat weapon and shield come back afterward.
- The best usable bag weapon is used (highest +, not broken, within your level). Identical copies of a weapon no longer block the swap. A weapon the game refuses to equip is not tried again.
- One-handed buff weapons keep your shield on; two-handed ones take it off and put it back. Keep one bag slot free.
- The Buffs & imbue page shows what each automatic buff is waiting for, or that no usable weapon is in your bag. **Wear the weapon each buff needs** switches this off; saved sets still work for buffs you assign to one.
- Every equipment move is still confirmed before the next step or the cast. Imbues and attack skills are not swapped.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server.
