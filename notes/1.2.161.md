# Start the bot when the character enters the game
- New option under Settings → Behaviour: Start the bot when this character enters the game (off by default, per character).
- About 5 seconds after entering: at the hunting spot it hunts; in the town of your saved town services it does them and reverses back; anywhere else it walks back; dead, it revives first.
- Once per entry. Stop all, F8 or a reconnect cancel it, so a bot you stopped stays stopped.
- Walks that start on a blocked tile or right against a wall now step out to open ground first, never through the wall.
- A town walk that finds no route goes to the town start once and tries the service again from there.
> Thanks to corevider for this one. Tested in simulation, not in a live game.
