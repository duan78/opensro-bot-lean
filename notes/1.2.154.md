# Repair hammer
- New option on the Supplies page: "Use a repair hammer when gear is worn". While hunting, when the most worn piece of gear you are wearing drops below the durability level you set (50% by default), the bot uses a Repair hammer from your bag. One hammer repairs everything you wear.
- It waits for the repair to show on the gear and stops after three unconfirmed attempts. If you also use the Town page's worn-gear trip, set the hammer level higher so the hammer comes first.
- Off by default.
> Contributed by Rebraxin (#33). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game: the hammer's item name and that it works as a plain item use are assumptions.
