# Pick Pet balancing and protected primary/secondary gear
- New "Pet and gear" box on the Items page. Pick a primary and secondary weapon and shield from what you wear or carry. Chosen gear is never sold, stored, dropped or moved to the pet.
- Buff swaps now wear the secondary weapon first when it fits the skill.
- New switch "Balance free slots with the pickup pet" (off by default). While hunting, items with a Sell or Store rule move one at a time to the bag or the pet, whichever has more free slots. Needs two free slots on the side that receives.
> Tested in simulation only. The pet transfer command is the one town trips already use; balancing on the live server has not been tried.
