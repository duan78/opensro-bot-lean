# Running low on gold no longer stops the bot in the potion shop
- If your gold does not cover the full potion refill, the bot now buys as many as the gold pays for and carries on, instead of stopping in the shop. This applies to town trips and to the walking restock.
- A potion you have none of and cannot afford still stops the trip with a "Not enough gold" message.
> Tested in simulation only. Found from a report where the bot stopped at the potion shop after "insufficient gold" errors; other causes were not ruled out.
