# Longer waits between page reloads
- The bot no longer stops after 3 page reloads in 30 minutes. Each reload in a row now waits twice as long as the one before.
- After a failed load the waits are 15 s, 30 s, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 min, then 30 min. After an expired session they start at 30 s.
- A small random extra delay (up to 20%) keeps many bots from reloading at the same moment after a server restart.
- The count resets once the character is back in the game. After 12 hours of reloads in a row without getting in, the bot stops and says so. The status line shows the wait.
> Contributed by corevider (#40). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
