# Avoid other players' targets, now by monster kind

- New "Avoided monsters" choice in Combat > Hunt area: All monsters (as before) or Normal & champion only.
- With Normal & champion only, a high-damage character still attacks party, giant, elite, titan and unique monsters another player is already fighting.
- The "another player is fighting it" skip count in the panel follows the same choice.
> Tested in the offline test suite only; not yet verified on a live server.
