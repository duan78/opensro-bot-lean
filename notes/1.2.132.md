# Berserk has its own Giant party switch
- **Combat -> Berserk** now has a **Giant party** switch, right under Giants. Berserk on party Giants follows it; other party monsters still follow **Party monsters**, so you can save Berserk for party Giants without spending it on every party mob.
- Saved profiles keep working: until you change it, **Giant party** takes whatever **Party monsters** was set to, so nobody loses Berserk on party Giants after updating. New profiles start with it off.
- Reported by Rebraxin (PR #11).
> Tested in simulation, and a player confirmed Berserk fired on party Giants on the live server.
