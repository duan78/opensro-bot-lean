# Pickup pet collects while the bot is stopped
- With a pickup pet set as collector, the pet now keeps picking up your wanted drops when the bot is off, using the same filters as while hunting.
- Only the pet does this: the character never walks to loot while the bot is stopped.
- The pet's settings are not changed when stopped. If its game auto-grab is on, the game collects natively; otherwise NIX sends the selective requests.
- Paused while a shop or the warehouse is open, and briefly after your own manual actions.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
