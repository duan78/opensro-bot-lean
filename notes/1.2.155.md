# Sell SoX gear too
- New Town page option: "Sell Star / Moon / Sun gear too (instead of depositing it)". When on, SoX gear from the pickup pet is sold at the repair merchant instead of going to the warehouse, and from your own bag as well when "Sell ordinary gear from the character bag too" is on.
- Keep names, buff-set gear, the alchemy weapon and the "also deposit gear requiring level" list still protect an item.
- Off by default.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
