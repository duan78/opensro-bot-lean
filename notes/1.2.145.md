# A full pet of protected items no longer strands you in town
- If the pickup pet is nearly full but everything in it is protected (nothing the trip can sell or store), the bot no longer goes to town for it, and a trip that is already under way carries on and goes back to hunting instead of stopping in town with "pet is full of protected items".
- A pet that holds sellable or storable items still triggers the trip as before.
> Tested in simulation only; reproduced from a player's diagnostics report, not on the live server.
