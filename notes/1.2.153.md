# Town trips work in low-level towns
- Low-level characters no longer get stuck in town when the potion merchant does not sell X-Large potions. The town cycle now buys the largest HP or MP potion that merchant does sell, and counts that potion for the low-stock check.
- Characters in towns that do sell X-Large potions behave exactly as before.
> Reported on Discord. Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
