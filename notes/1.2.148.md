# Holy Spell and Body Blessing can now be kept up
- Cleric blessings such as Holy Spell and Body Blessing now show in the Buffs list and are cast on yourself. Before, the list hid every skill that asks for a target, so they never appeared.
- Works with buff gear swaps too. Healing skills that only target other players still stay in the Party tab.
> Tested in simulation only; not tried on the live server.
