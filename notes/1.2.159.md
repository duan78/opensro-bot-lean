# SoX filter on the Items page, steadier status box
- Items page: a new SoX filter in Choose items shows only Star, Moon or Sun gear (or any SoX). The bulk buttons apply to what it shows.
- The status lines under HP/MP now sit in a box of fixed height, so changing text no longer pushes the rest of the panel around. Long text scrolls inside the box, and hovering it shows the full text.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
