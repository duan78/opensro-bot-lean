# Town trips that start in town no longer waste a Return Scroll
- If the bot starts a town trip while you are already standing in town (for example you start hunting there with the town cycle on), it now walks straight to the services. It no longer uses a Return Scroll, and no longer needs one in the bag.
- After the trip it uses the reverse scroll to go back to your last scroll point, or your hunting area if there is none, and hunting resumes there.
> Reported on Discord by egecan (#22). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. It relies on your town services being saved and within about 500 m of where you stand.
