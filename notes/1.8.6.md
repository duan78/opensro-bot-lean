# Local audit fixes: quest data, buff-gear recovery, privacy

- The quest catalogue can load again: questData.json and npcpos.json were missing from the asset worker's allowlist, so the picked-quests list always said the data could not be loaded. A failed load now also waits ten minutes before trying again instead of retrying ten times a second.
- A death or world change in the middle of a buff weapon swap no longer strands the bot: hunting restarts on its own after the walk back while the combat gear restores itself, instead of sitting idle at the spot. The automatic restore also stays quiet during the town trip instead of repeating its warning ten times a second, and waits out open trades and stalls instead of failing one tick later.
- A revive command the server never answers is asked again every ten seconds; before, the bot could stay dead forever.
- A unique converge (team edition) can no longer write one character's hunting spot into another character's profile: the saved spot goes back before the profile changes, and a stale converge is dropped.
- The learned small-town potion tier is cleared when the character or profile changes, so one character's potion refuels do not drive another's.
- Privacy: the character's name no longer reaches uploaded bug reports through the reconnect message, and a relay connection error never carries the room key.
- The "pet bag is full" warning no longer depends on the chime being on; Max every skill stops its fast loop when the game world is left; the panel keeps updating when one status line breaks; a setting change made just before closing the page is still saved; the report rate-limit wait survives a page reload.

> All changes were verified with the offline test suite only (719 tests), not in a live game.
