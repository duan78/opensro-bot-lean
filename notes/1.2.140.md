# Dying during a buff weapon swap no longer leaves the buff weapon on
- If your character died (or the world changed) while the bot was wearing a buff weapon, the bot used to stop hunting and leave that weapon on, so after reviving you could not attack until you pressed "Restore combat gear". Now hunting carries on, and the bot puts your combat weapon and shield back on by itself as soon as the character is alive again, before the return trip.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server.
