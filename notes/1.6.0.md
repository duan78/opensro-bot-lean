# Attack pet assist
- New, off by default, under Combat → Hunt area: every monster the bot engages is also given to the attack pet, with the same order the game's own pet bar sends. The pet stops staying behind on an old monster while you fight a new one.
- With the call-back on (default), a pet farther than the radius (15 m) is first called back to you, then sets out to the new target from your side — whatever chases it comes along, a walking lure. Between targets the pet stays at your side. The wait gives up after a timeout (3 s) and orders the attack anyway; the order repeats every few seconds (5 s) so a lost one never leaves the pet idle.
- Only your own living attack pet is commanded; the pickup pet keeps collecting beside this, transports are untouched, and nothing is sent while a town trip, a restock, a walk-back or a resurrection owns the character, or while the bot is stopped.
> Verified in simulation only (world-mirror tests); not yet exercised against the live server.
