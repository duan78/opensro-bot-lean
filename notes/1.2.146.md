# A blocked walk to the warehouse no longer ends the town trip
- If the bot cannot walk around an obstacle on the way to the warehouse, it now skips the deposit and carries on with potions, repairs and the way back, instead of stopping in town with "Blocked on the way to the warehouse".
- A blocked walk to the other town services still stops the trip, as before.
> Tested in simulation only. The cause of the block itself (why the character stopped moving) is not known; the bot's route from the town arrival point to the Hotan warehouse checks out against the game's collision data.
