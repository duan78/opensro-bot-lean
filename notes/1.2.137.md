# Automatic trading between towns
- New **Auto trade** page. Pick the town to buy in and the town to sell in, press **Start trading**, and the bot does the whole run: summons your transport, buys the dearest trade goods you can afford at the trade merchant, walks to the other town, and sells everything there. It reports the gold gained.
- **Deliveries** sets how many runs to do (0 = until you stop it). With more than one, the bot buys the second town's goods and carries them back.
- Potions keep being used on the road, and at each town the bot first tops up the potions from your Supplies page targets.
- Needs your trader job suit on and a transport scroll in the bag (or a transport already summoned), plus enough gold. Walking only, no teleports. Bandits are not fought; the run stops with a message if you die, take manual control, or the merchant does not link to your transport.
- Towns: Jangan, Donwhang, Hotan, Samarkand and Constantinople.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server. The bot cannot yet buy a transport for you.
